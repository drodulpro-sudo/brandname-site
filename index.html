Our story

A roastery built around one stubborn idea: fresh means fresh.

Most coffee is roasted in huge volumes, bagged, and left to wait — in a warehouse, on a pallet, on a shelf — long after its best flavours have faded. We started Ember & Oak to do the opposite.

From our roastery at 14 Kiln Yard in Portland, we roast in small batches every week. Each batch gets our full attention: we listen for first crack, watch the colour, taste every roast, and adjust. Then it's bagged and out the door within 48 hours.

Why Ember & Oak

Coffee has a peak. We ship at it.

Roasted coffee is at its most vibrant in the days and weeks after roasting. Our whole operation — batch size, schedule, shipping — is built to get it to you inside that window.

  1. Day 0

    Roasted in a small batch

    Every week, not every quarter. Small batches mean we can taste and adjust each roast instead of running a one-size recipe.

  2. Within 48 hours

    Packed & shipped

    Your coffee leaves the roastery within 48 hours of roasting. It never sits on a warehouse pallet waiting for an order.

  3. On arrival

    In your cup at its peak

    Brighter acidity, fuller aroma, sweeter finish. The tasting notes on the bag are the notes you'll actually taste.
